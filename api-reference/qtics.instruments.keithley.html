Bases: DCSource, SCPIInstrument

Keithley Model 2231A-30-3 Triple Channel DC Power Supply by Keithley Instruments.

voltage, current and their limits act on the channel selected with channel. The selected channel is state of the device, shared by all the properties: changing it from several threads is not thread-safe.
