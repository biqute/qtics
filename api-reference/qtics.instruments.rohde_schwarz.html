class qtics.instruments.rohde_schwarz.sma100b.SMA100B(transport: Transport | str, name: str = 'SMA100B', **transport_kwargs)[source]#

Bases: RFSource, SCPIInstrument

R&S SMA100B RF and microwave signal generator by Rohde & Schwarz.

The RF level readback depends on the power unit set with p_unit. To comply with the RFSource interface (power in dBm), the unit is set to dBm right after connecting. p_unit stays available, but changing it makes power read and write in that unit.

Frequencies are the displayed ones, f_rf * f_mult + f_offset: their range follows the multiplier and offset of a downstream instrument. These are read when connecting and tracked by the f_mult/f_offset properties (a change from the front panel is seen after reading them or reconnecting).
