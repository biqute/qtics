Common interfaces of instrument types.

Submodules#

qtics.instruments.types.source module#

Sources: RF generators and DC sources.

Optional capabilities (e.g. power of an RF source, current of a DC source) are documented here and implemented only by the drivers supporting them.

qtics.instruments.types.swept module#

Swept analyzers: vector network analyzers and spectrum analyzers.

Frequencies are in Hz, powers in dBm. f_start, f_stop, f_center and f_span are coupled: setting one changes the others.
